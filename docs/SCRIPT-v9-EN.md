Lines 1-7 keep the owner's words and order. Line 5 stays unchanged until the owner answers on Option A. Lines 8 and 11 and the cards follow the owner's Batch 19 answers: "Cards, and voice in line 8 (Recommended)", "Match the first cards (Recommended)", "Night landing (Recommended)". Structure: "consult with barbot"; BarBot and the director agree on one man, one clock: an anchor and exactly three returns, each escalating.

Every new line is fictional game dialogue and never shown as a quote.

Cards: "Tel Aviv. 13 May 1948." at 0 s. "David Ben-Gurion. Leader of the Jews of Palestine." on line 4. After line 9: "14 May 1948, 4 p.m. The State of Israel is declared." then "Thirteen hours later. Egyptian planes bomb Tel Aviv."

0. [Anchor: desk, lamp, the old hand winds the clock, tick starts. Card.]
1. The Jewish people has been exiled from its homeland, without a home for two thousand years.
2. Persecuted. Attacked. Humiliated.
3. And murdered on an industrial scale.
R1. [Return 1, after the train: the hand at the clock.]
4. Now we have a chance to get back our homeland. And finally have a country of our own. [Night landing]
5. But the stakes are high. Since the UN declaration of partition, the Arabs have attacked us relentlessly.
6. And five Arab armies are now about to invade.
R2. [Return 2: he stands at the shutter, the shadows of men argue on the wall. No map.]
7. We don't have enough weapons. Enough men. Enough food.
8. Tomorrow at midnight, British rule ends. Washington says wait. Declare a state now, and we stand alone. My operations chief gives us an even chance at best. My own colleagues are divided.
R3. [Return 3: he walks to the window. Slams the shutter. The tick dies on line 9.]
9. We do not wait.
10. [Silence. Bomb, blast, Tel Aviv burns.]
11. One army, or three? The guns: Jerusalem, or the Jordan Valley? Give your first order.
